Experiment Title
Soil Texture Test
Learning Objective
Enable students to determine soil texture by observing how soil particles settle in water according to particle size and density.
1. Overview of the Virtual Experiment
The learner performs a simulated soil texture analysis using a virtual transparent jar filled with soil and water. The experiment visually demonstrates sedimentation and separation into layers:
	Sand → settles first 
	Silt → settles second 
	Clay → settles last 
Students can:
	Select different soil samples 
	Shake the jar 
	Watch settling over time 
	Measure layer thicknesses 
	Identify soil texture type

11. Sample Conclusion in the Virtual Lab
Different soil samples contain different proportions of sand, silt, and clay. Larger particles such as sand settle faster while smaller clay particles settle last.
